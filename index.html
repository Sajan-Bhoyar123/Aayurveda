About Umed Care Centre

The Standard of Care Your Loved One Has Always Deserved.

Caring for an aging, sick, or bedridden parent is a deeply emotional responsibility. When physical requirements exceed what can be safely managed at home, Umed Care Centre steps in as an extension of your own family.

Our nurses and attendants are professionally trained, background-verified, and assigned with consistency to ensure residents receive attentive, compassionate, and personalised care. With 24×7 CCTV surveillance, emergency call bells in every room, hourly night nursing rounds, and additional monitoring whenever required, we prioritise the safety, comfort, and well-being of every resident. Visiting physicians conduct regular health reviews to ensure appropriate medical supervision.

“With 24×7 CCTV surveillance, hourly night nursing rounds, additional checks whenever needed, and regular physician visits, we strive to help residents feel safe and families feel confident, relieved, and supported.”

— Medical & Administration Team, Umed Care Centre

Our Founder's Vision

A Message from Founder

“Every Life Deserves Care, Dignity, and Hope.”

When I began this journey 26 years ago, I had one simple belief — no individual should feel helpless, forgotten, or alone at a time when they need care the most.

Over the years, I have witnessed the challenges faced by individuals who are unable to manage their daily lives independently, as well as the emotional struggles of families who want to provide the best possible care for their loved ones.

These experiences have shaped my purpose and strengthened my commitment to Umeed Care Centre.

For me, Umeed has never been just an institution. It is a responsibility, a commitment, and a place where compassion meets care.

Our journey has not always been easy. There have been challenges, difficult moments, and countless responsibilities. But every small improvement in someone’s comfort, every reassuring smile, and every family that places its trust in us reminds me why this journey matters.

Today, Umeed provides support to elderly individuals and people living with conditions such as paralysis, dementia, Alzheimer’s, prolonged immobility, and other situations requiring assisted or dependent care.

But beyond these services, what matters most to me is how we treat every individual — with patience, respect, understanding, and dignity.

I am deeply grateful to the dedicated team that stands beside me and to the families who have trusted Umeed over the years. Their support has helped turn a simple vision into a meaningful journey of 26 years.

Our Core Conviction

“Care is not merely about helping someone live. It is about making them feel valued, respected, and never alone.”

As we move forward, my commitment is to continue nurturing Umeed with the same compassion, dedication, and sense of responsibility with which this journey began.

Because to me, Umeed is more than a name. It is hope, and hope deserves to be kept alive.

Dr. Ranjana Kadam-Powar

Founder, Umeed Care Centre

Clinical Care Standards

Dedicated Condition Protocols: Paralyzed, Dementia & Bedsores

Structured, doctor-guided protocols designed to maintain clinical stability, skin integrity, cognitive peace, and dignity for high-dependency residents.

Mobility & Rehabilitation 01

Paralyzed Care Protocol

Specialized assistance for stroke recovery, partial or full paralysis, and spinal mobility restrictions.

  • Safe Transfers: 2-person assisted bed-to-wheelchair transfers.
  • Passive Physiotherapy: Daily limb exercises to prevent muscle contractures.
  • Posture & Alignment: Pillow positioning to relieve joint pressure.
  • Hygiene Assistance: Bedside sponge baths, catheter & diaper care.
Limb Care & Mobility • Daily Protocol
Cognitive Safety 02

Dementia Care Protocol

Compassionate, patient-centered framework for individuals with dementia, memory loss, and Alzheimer's.

  • Calm Daily Routines: Predictable daytime schedules to prevent confusion.
  • 24/7 Safety Supervision: Round-the-clock monitoring and wandering prevention.
  • Empathetic Communication: Patient verbal cues with gentle reassurance.
  • Assisted Living: Patient guidance for meals, dressing, and hygiene.
Memory & Safety • 24/7 Monitored
Skin Integrity & Healing 03

Bedsore Management Protocol

Intensive clinical protocol for preventing and managing pressure ulcers in bedridden residents.

  • Strict 2-Hour Turning: Scheduled lateral left, back, and right turning.
  • Air & Water Mattresses: Continuous pressure relief across pressure points.
  • Sterile Antiseptic Dressing: Daily cleaning, medication, and wound care.
  • Nutritional Healing: High-protein diet and skin barrier creams.
Wound & Skin Care • 2-Hour Turning
