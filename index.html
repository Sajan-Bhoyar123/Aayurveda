About Umed Care Centre

The Standard of Care Your Loved One Has Always Deserved.

Caring for an aging, sick, or bedridden parent is a deeply emotional responsibility. When physical requirements exceed what can be safely managed at home, Umed Care Centre steps in as an extension of your own family.

Our nurses and attendants are professionally trained, background-verified, and assigned with consistency — so our residents never feel like they are in an impersonal clinic or being attended to by strangers.

"Emergency call bells in every room, 24×7 CCTV, night nursing rounds every two hours, and a visiting physician who reviews every resident regularly. We want families to feel confident, relieved, and supported."

— Medical & Administration Team, Umed Care Centre
