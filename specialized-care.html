Specialized Clinical Programs

Care Designed Around Individual Needs

At Umed Care Centre, we provide assisted and long-term care for individuals who require continuous support because of age, reduced mobility, neurological conditions, prolonged illness, or dependence on others for everyday activities.

Our focus goes beyond accommodation. We aim to provide a safe, supportive, and dignified environment where each resident’s day-to-day needs can be managed with personal attention, expert clinical oversight, and sincere compassion.

Conditions We Support

Dedicated Pathways for Every Dependency Level

Every resident receives personalized assistance and structured monitoring aligned with their unique condition and medical diagnosis.

Category 01 01

Old Age & Assisted Elder Care

Support for seniors who need assistance with everyday living.

As people grow older, activities such as walking, bathing, dressing, eating, maintaining personal hygiene, or managing a daily routine may become difficult.

At Umed, support includes assistance with daily activities, meals, mobility, personal hygiene, comfort, companionship, and routine care based on the individual’s needs.

Personal Attention • Loving Companionship
Category 02 02

Bedridden & Dependent Care

Dedicated support for individuals who cannot manage daily activities independently.

Bedridden individuals may require frequent assistance with positioning, hygiene, meals, comfort, and other everyday needs.

Our care approach focuses on maintaining comfort, cleanliness, dignity, and attentive day-to-day support while reducing complications associated with prolonged immobility where possible.

Repositioning Care • Hygiene Details
Category 03 03

Paralysis Care

Assistance for individuals living with partial or significant loss of mobility.

People affected by paralysis may require help with movement, transfers, and everyday personal activities.

Umed provides supportive assistance according to the resident’s level of dependency, with an emphasis on safe mobility, daily routines, personal care, comfort, and dignity.

Safe Transfers • Dignified Routines
Category 04 04

Dementia Care

A supportive environment for individuals experiencing memory and cognitive difficulties.

Dementia can affect memory, communication, orientation, and the ability to perform everyday activities.

Our approach emphasizes familiar routines, patient communication, supervision, assistance with daily activities, and a calm, respectful environment tailored to the person’s needs.

Cognitive Calm • 24/7 Supervision
Category 05 05

Alzheimer’s Care

Compassionate assistance for individuals living with Alzheimer’s disease.

As Alzheimer’s progresses, a person may increasingly need support with communication, eating, hygiene, mobility, and other daily activities.

Umed focuses on providing consistent routines, supervision, companionship, and personal assistance while treating every individual with patience and respect.

Consistent Routines • Constant Companion
Category 06 06

Coma & Highly Dependent Care

Continuous supportive care for individuals with a high level of dependency.

Individuals in a coma or with severe dependency may require extensive day-to-day assistance and close coordination with their treating medical professionals.

Umed provides supportive care according to the individual’s requirements and our available capabilities, with attention to hygiene, positioning, nutrition support, comfort, and general care routines.

Medical Oversight • Tube Feed Care
Category 07

Bedsore / Pressure Sore Care

Focused care for individuals at risk of or living with pressure sores.

People who remain in one position for extended periods can be at risk of developing pressure injuries.

Care includes regular repositioning every 2 hours, hygiene and skin care, wound-care support as clinically appropriate, nutrition attention, air/water bed usage, and coordination with medical professionals when required.

2-Hour Repositioning Air & Water Mattresses Daily Antiseptic Dressing Specialized Skin Hygiene
Pre-Admission Assessment

A Care Plan Built Around the Person

Every resident comes to Umed with a different history, condition, and level of dependency. Before admission, the person’s care requirements and Umed’s ability to safely support those requirements are thoroughly assessed.

1

Clinical Assessment

Detailed evaluation of resident’s medical history, current prescriptions, mobility level, and mental orientation.

2

Custom Protocol Creation

Setting up specific dietary instructions, repositioning schedules, doctor visit intervals, and vitals check frequency.

3

Attentive Daily Care

Assignment of dedicated, consistent nurses and attendants with transparent weekly progress updates to the family.
