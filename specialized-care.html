Umeed Care Center Pune

Thoughtfully Designed Elder & Bedridden Care in Pune

At Umeed Care Center Pune, we specialize in advanced medical care, 24/7 nursing supervision, and clinical assistance for bedridden, paralyzed, and disabled individuals. Serving in this field since September 2000 with over 26 years of clinical healthcare experience. This is a project of OM Prathishthan Charitable Trust.

Our clinical facility is fully equipped to handle emergency cases 24/7 and provides dedicated on-site oxygen backup, specialized bedsore management, nasal feeding tube care, and structured protocols for paralysis, dementia, Alzheimer's, and coma patients.

Unlike a generic old age home, Umed functions as a dedicated healthcare sanctuary where professional nursing, daily doctor visits, and continuous clinical oversight ensure safety, medical stability, and dignity for every resident.

Conditions We Support

Dedicated Pathways for Every Dependency Level

Every resident receives personalized assistance and structured monitoring aligned with their unique condition and medical diagnosis.

Category 01 01

Old Age & Assisted Elder Care

Support for seniors who need assistance with everyday living.

As people grow older, activities such as walking, bathing, dressing, eating, maintaining personal hygiene, or managing a daily routine may become difficult.

At Umed, support includes assistance with daily activities, meals, mobility, personal hygiene, comfort, companionship, and routine care based on the individual’s needs.

Personal Attention • Loving Companionship
Category 02 02

Bedridden & Dependent Care

Dedicated medical and nursing support for individuals who cannot manage daily activities independently due to paralysis, fractures, coma, neurological conditions, or prolonged immobility.

Our care focuses on maintaining comfort, hygiene, dignity, and overall well-being through personalised assistance with everyday needs.

Daily Living & Personal Care Services:

  • Sponging Bath & Hygiene Care – Assistance with bathing, personal hygiene, and cleanliness.
  • Feeding Assistance – Support with meals and daily nutritional needs.
  • Nasal Feeding Tube Support – Skilled nutrition and feeding care for individuals who require a nasal feeding tube, as prescribed.
  • Dressing & Grooming – Assistance with changing clothes and personal grooming.
  • Physiotherapy Support – Assistance with prescribed exercises and mobility care.
  • Repositioning & Bed Care – Regular repositioning and comfort support to help reduce complications associated with prolonged immobility.

Our qualified nurses and ward boys provide compassionate, personalised care around the clock.

Repositioning & Nasal Tube Care • 24/7 Monitored
Category 03 • Protocol 03

Paralyzed Care Protocol

Structured clinical assistance for individuals living with stroke, partial, or severe loss of mobility.

People affected by paralysis require specialized help with transfers, limb positioning, passive movement, and personal hygiene.

Clinical Protocol Standards:

• 2-person assisted transfers to prevent falls

• Daily passive range-of-motion (ROM) physiotherapy

• Pillow-assisted limb positioning to prevent contractures

• Dignified bedside hygiene, sponge baths & diaper care

Safe Transfers • Dignified Routines
Category 04 • Protocol 04

Dementia Care Protocol

A secure, calm, and structured environment for individuals experiencing memory and cognitive challenges.

Dementia can affect memory, orientation, and emotional comfort. Our specialized protocol provides consistent care and round-the-clock safety.

Clinical Protocol Standards:

• Predictable daytime routines to reduce anxiety

• 24/7 continuous monitoring & wandering prevention

• Gentle, empathetic communication techniques

• Attentive guidance for eating, dressing & hygiene

Cognitive Calm • 24/7 Supervision
Category 05 05

Alzheimer’s Care

Compassionate assistance for individuals living with Alzheimer’s disease.

As Alzheimer’s progresses, a person may increasingly need support with communication, eating, hygiene, mobility, and other daily activities.

Umed focuses on providing consistent routines, supervision, companionship, and personal assistance while treating every individual with patience and respect.

Consistent Routines • Constant Companion
Category 06 06

Coma & Highly Dependent Care

Continuous supportive care for individuals with a high level of dependency.

Individuals in a coma or with severe dependency may require extensive day-to-day assistance and close coordination with their treating medical professionals.

Umed provides supportive care according to the individual’s requirements and our available capabilities, with attention to hygiene, positioning, nutrition support, comfort, and general care routines.

Medical Oversight • Nasal Feeding Tube Care
Category 07 • Protocol

Bedsore Management Protocol

Intensive clinical protocol for preventing, dressing, and healing pressure sores in bedridden individuals.

People who remain in one position for extended periods are at high risk of skin breakdown. Our dedicated protocol ensures rigorous skin care and proactive prevention.

Care includes strict repositioning every 2 hours, sterile antiseptic wound dressing, medical air and water bed mattresses, skin barrier protection, and high-protein healing nutrition.

Strict 2-Hour Turning Air & Water Mattresses Daily Antiseptic Dressing High-Protein Healing Diet
Pre-Admission Assessment

A Care Plan Built Around the Person

Every resident comes to Umed with a different history, condition, and level of dependency. Before admission, the person’s care requirements and Umed’s ability to safely support those requirements are thoroughly assessed.

1

Clinical Assessment

Detailed evaluation of resident’s medical history, current prescriptions, mobility level, and mental orientation.

2

Custom Protocol Creation

Setting up specific dietary instructions, repositioning schedules, doctor visit intervals, and vitals check frequency.

3

Attentive Daily Care

Assignment of dedicated, consistent nurses and attendants with transparent weekly progress updates to the family.
